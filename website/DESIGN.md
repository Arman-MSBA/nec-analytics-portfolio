# Portfolio presentation

The selected direction combines Concept A's evidence-first homepage with Concept C's case-study structure. The audience includes analytics, business intelligence, analytics engineering and applied AI hiring teams, with consulting as a secondary path.

- Use white space, navy type, restrained teal actions, and pale blue supporting panels.
- Let original charts and documented workflows carry the visual emphasis.
- Keep the opening introduction short and show BI, ML and AI together.
- Begin case studies with the artifact, question, contribution and finding. Put detailed methods, files and limitations within the same narrative.
- Keep academic exercises distinct from employment work. Show source-qualified results and concrete professional deliverables; avoid invented business impact.
- Use native controls, readable type, visible keyboard focus and responsive layouts. Static screenshots have original-image links; workbook interactions are available through the downloaded Tableau packages.

The native Tableau images are original 1247 × 863 screenshots. CSS crops only their presentation; it does not redraw charts. The original captures and downloadable workbooks remain available. Generated concept images are design references and are not used as evidence on the published site.
